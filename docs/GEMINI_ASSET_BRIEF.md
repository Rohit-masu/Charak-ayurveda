## PASTE FROM HERE: style guide (paste first, every chat)

```
You are generating photographic concept visuals for Charak Ayurveda, a premium Indian Ayurvedic medicine brand. Every image in this set must look like it came from one photoshoot.

Look and mood: modern, premium, calm, clinical-clean and trustworthy, quietly rooted in Ayurveda. Think a high-end apothecary or a modern pharmacy brand campaign, not a spa and not a temple.

Colour palette (stay inside it):
- Warm ivory #F7F3EC and soft beige #EEE6D9 backgrounds
- Sandstone / travertine #DCCDB6 surfaces
- Deep warm charcoal #1E1C1A
- Muted bronze / antique gold #9C7848 accents (matte metal, never shiny glitter)
- Product colours: Rati Shakti Men = matte black #121212 with bronze; Rati Shakti Women = muted rose #B4636B and deep rose #5E2832 with bronze

Materials: matte paper and card, travertine or sandstone stone, brushed brass/bronze, unbleached linen, amber glass, small amounts of dried herbs, roots and spices (ashwagandha root, saffron threads, cardamom, dried rose petals). Botanicals are a small supporting detail, never the subject.

Lighting: soft directional daylight from the left at about 45°, like a large window. Gentle long soft shadows, warm white balance (about 4800K), low contrast, slightly lifted blacks, low saturation, fine film-like grain.

Camera: photographic realism, 85mm lens look, eye level or slightly above, shallow depth of field, clean negative space for website text.

Always avoid: heavy green foliage, leaf piles, gardens, forests, lush plants; mandalas, temples, deities, diyas, incense smoke clichés; people, hands, faces, couples, bodies, any romantic or sensual imagery; hearts; hospital or pill-spill pharma clichés; shiny gold glitter, gradients, neon, lens flares, bokeh balls; glassmorphism and CGI plastic look; any readable text except what the prompt allows; logos of other brands; certification marks, FSSAI/AYUSH/GMP seals, badges, "100%"/"No.1" stamps, medical claims; watermarks, borders, frames, collages.
```
